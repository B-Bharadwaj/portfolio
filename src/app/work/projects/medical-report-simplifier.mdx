## Overview

A tool that converts complex medical reports into plain English that patients can actually understand — without losing critical medical meaning or safety-critical negations ("no evidence of cancer" must never become "evidence of cancer").

## Key Features

- **Gemini 2.5 Flash Pipeline**: PDF parsing → LLM simplification → safety evaluation → output, with automatic rewrites for unsafe simplifications.
- **Safety Evaluation Layer**: Three-part check — negation detection, critical medical term retention, and semantic similarity scoring against the original.
- **Auto-Rewrite Module**: If a simplification fails safety checks, the system automatically generates a corrected version rather than passing through unsafe output.
- **Readability Improvement**: Reduced Flesch-Kincaid readability grade from 14–16 (graduate level) down to 7–9 (middle school level).
- **100% Negation Safety**: Zero negation errors across all test cases.

## Technologies Used

- **Gemini 2.5 Flash** — LLM for medical text simplification
- **Python** — Pipeline orchestration
- **NLP libraries** — Negation detection and semantic similarity
- **PDF parsing** — Extracting structured text from medical documents

## Challenges & Learnings

Medical text simplification is a high-stakes NLP problem. The key insight was that you can't just check readability — you need explicit safety constraints. Building the negation detection and auto-rewrite loop was the most interesting engineering challenge.
