## Overview

SENN automatically discovers, optimises, and compresses CNN architectures for image classification using evolutionary algorithms — eliminating manual architecture design entirely. Each CNN is encoded as a deterministic Architecture DNA and evolved over multiple generations using mutation, multi-objective selection, and structured pruning.

## What I Did

- Encoded each CNN as a JSON-based Architecture DNA with configurable conv layers (2–6), filters (16–128), kernel sizes, activations, pooling, and regularisation — ensuring valid, reproducible architectures across generations.
- Implemented a mutation engine introducing controlled structural changes — adding/removing layers, adjusting filters, toggling BatchNorm/Dropout — all constraint-aware to prevent invalid CNNs.
- Applied NSGA-II Pareto selection with non-dominated sorting and crowding distance, simultaneously optimising for validation accuracy and model efficiency (parameters, FLOPs).
- Used short 2–3 epoch training per model during evolution to efficiently evaluate 48 architectures across 6 generations (8 models per generation), followed by full 30–50 epoch training on the best candidates.
- Built a Streamlit dashboard for live tracking of fitness curves, architecture mutations, and Pareto frontiers across generations.

## Results

- CIFAR-10 accuracy: ~75–78% on fully trained best architectures
- Reduced parameters and FLOPs compared to baseline CNNs
- 48 architectures evaluated across 6 generations (8 models per generation) without human intervention

## Technologies Used

- **PyTorch** — Model construction and training
- **NSGA-II** — Multi-objective evolutionary optimisation
- **Streamlit** — Live evolution tracking dashboard
- **NumPy** — Genetic operators (mutation, selection)
- **CIFAR-10** — Benchmark dataset

## Challenges & Learnings

Balancing exploration versus exploitation was the core challenge — too much mutation destabilised good architectures, too little stalled diversity. NSGA-II's crowding distance naturally maintained population diversity without manual tuning. Excluding crossover and weight inheritance was a deliberate choice to preserve architectural validity and deterministic reconstruction.
